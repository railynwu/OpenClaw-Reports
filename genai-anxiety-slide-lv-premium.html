The AI Anxiety Paradox

FEAR

AI is TOO STRONG

Traditional industries under threat

Lawyers, analysts, insurance, wealth managers, logistics—any role touching AI faces disruption.

Market reaction: Capital flees. Stocks get hammered.

DOUBT

AI is NOT STRONG ENOUGH

Tech giants burning cash

Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Google: $600B+ capex in 2026.

Problem: Profits haven't exploded. Cash flow devoured. Returns unclear.

KEY INSIGHT

Classic "expectation squeeze" phase. Over-optimism → Bubble doubt → Emotional stampede → Back to fundamentals.

Real question: Who survives to reap rewards? Long-term winners turn AI into profits.
