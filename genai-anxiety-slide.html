The AI Anxiety Paradox

😰
Fear: AI is TOO Strong
Traditional industries under threat
Lawyers, financial analysts, insurance brokers, wealth managers, logistics—any role "touching AI" faces disruption.

Market reaction: Capital flees first. Related stocks get hammered.
VS
🤔
Doubt: AI is NOT Strong Enough
Tech giants burning cash
Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Google: $600B+ capex expected in 2026.

The problem: Profits haven't exploded yet, but cash flow is being "devoured." Returns? Unclear.
💡 Key Insight
This is a classic "expectation squeeze" phase. Every tech revolution goes through: Over-optimism → Bubble doubt → Emotional stampede → Back to fundamentals.

The real question isn't "Will AI work?"—it's "Who survives to reap the rewards?" Short-term volatility will be intense. Long-term winners won't be the best storytellers, but those who turn AI into profits.
